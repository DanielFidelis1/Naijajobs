Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how NaijaJobs collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you use our platform.

Effective date: 12 November 2026

1. Who we are

NaijaJobs is an online platform operated by Daniel Fidelis-Irile Oshioke. It connects people looking for work with individuals and organisations seeking workers.

In this Policy, "NaijaJobs", "we", "our" and "us" refer to the platform and its operator. "You" refers to anyone who accesses or uses NaijaJobs.

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable Nigerian data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2. Personal information we collect

Depending on how you use NaijaJobs, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Your full name, username, email address, password credentials and account type.
  • Profile information: Your location, skills, biography and profile picture, where provided.
  • Job information: Job titles, descriptions, categories, budgets, locations, deadlines and job status.
  • Application information: Applications, cover messages, application status and work-completion confirmations.
  • Communication information: Messages exchanged through platform messaging and information submitted through support requests.
  • Reviews and ratings: Ratings, comments and feedback submitted by eligible users.
  • Payment information: Payment references, transaction status, payment amounts and information needed to manage paid platform features.
  • Technical information: Information that may be generated when you use the website, such as browser details, device information, IP address, access logs and security-related records.

We do not intend to collect more personal information than is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy.

3. How we collect information

We may collect personal information when you:

  • Create an account or update your profile.
  • Post a job or apply for available work.
  • Communicate with another user through NaijaJobs.
  • Submit a review, rating or support request.
  • Purchase a premium feature or subscription.
  • Visit or interact with our website.

We may also receive relevant transaction information from payment service providers when you make a payment.

4. Why we use your information

We may process personal information to:

  • Create, manage and secure user accounts.
  • Display profiles and connect Clients with Workers.
  • Allow Clients to post jobs and Workers to apply.
  • Manage applications, job statuses and completion confirmations.
  • Enable eligible users to communicate through platform messaging.
  • Display reviews and ratings associated with completed work.
  • Process and verify payments for paid platform features.
  • Provide customer support and respond to complaints.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, suspicious activity and security threats.
  • Enforce our Terms & Conditions and platform rules.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve the platform.
  • Meet legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

5. Legal basis for processing

Where required by applicable data protection law, we rely on an appropriate legal basis to process personal information. Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide account services and features you request.
  • Consent: Processing where you have freely given permission for a specific purpose.
  • Legitimate interests: Processing necessary for appropriate platform operations, security, fraud prevention and service improvement, where your rights do not override those interests.
  • Legal obligations: Processing required to comply with applicable laws or lawful requests.

6. How information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers. However, information may be shared where necessary to operate NaijaJobs and provide its services.

  • Other users: Certain profile details, job information, applications, reviews and messages may be visible to users who are permitted to access the relevant platform feature.
  • Service providers: We may use third-party providers for hosting, database services, authentication, payment processing, email delivery and technical operations.
  • Payment providers: Payment information needed to complete or verify transactions may be shared with Paystack or another payment provider used by NaijaJobs.
  • Legal authorities: We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to respond to valid legal processes and protect rights or safety.
  • Business changes: Information may be transferred as part of a lawful business restructuring or transfer, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

7. Information visible to other users

NaijaJobs is designed to connect Clients and Workers. As a result, some information may be visible to other users when you use the platform.

For example, profile details may be displayed to help users evaluate one another. Job postings may be visible to users browsing available jobs. Reviews may be displayed where the platform permits them.

Messaging is subject to platform access rules. Users should avoid including passwords, banking credentials or other highly confidential information in messages or public profile content.

8. Payments and financial information

NaijaJobs may offer paid features such as Featured Jobs, Worker Premium and Client Subscription plans.

Payments may be processed by Paystack or another payment provider made available by NaijaJobs. These providers may collect and process payment information under their own privacy policies and terms.

NaijaJobs may receive transaction references, payment confirmations, amounts and payment status information needed to verify purchases and activate eligible features.

We do not intend to store your complete card number or card security code on NaijaJobs servers. Payment details should be handled through the payment provider's secure payment process.

9. Data storage and security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

These measures may include access controls, authentication, security monitoring and appropriate restrictions on access to user information.

However, no website, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their passwords and notify us if they suspect unauthorised access to their accounts.

10. How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information, the user's account status, outstanding transactions, security requirements and legal obligations.

When an account is deleted, we take steps to remove associated information where appropriate. Some records may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution or other lawful purposes.

11. Your data protection rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information in appropriate circumstances.
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Object to processing where the law gives you that right.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on your consent.
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format where applicable.
  • Complain to the relevant data protection authority.

Some rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

12. Account deletion and data requests

You may request deletion of your NaijaJobs account through the available account deletion process or by contacting our support team.

We will handle deletion requests in accordance with applicable law. Information that must be retained for legal, security, financial or dispute-resolution purposes may be kept for the appropriate period.

13. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website or the services used to operate it may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to support essential website functions, remember settings, maintain sessions and improve security.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect the operation of some website features.

14. International data transfers

Some of our service providers may process or store information on systems located outside Nigeria.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take steps required by applicable data protection law to ensure that the transfer has an appropriate legal basis and safeguards.

15. Children's privacy

NaijaJobs is intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly invite children under 18 to create accounts or use the platform.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a person who is not eligible to use the platform, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

16. Third-party websites and services

NaijaJobs may contain links to third-party websites or use services provided by other organisations. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

We encourage users to review the privacy information provided by third-party services before sharing personal information with them.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, operating practices or legal obligations change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Where a change materially affects users, we will take reasonable steps to notify them where appropriate.

18. Complaints and regulatory rights

If you have a concern about how NaijaJobs handles your personal information, please contact us first so we can review and respond to your concern.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), subject to its applicable procedures.
