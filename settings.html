Delete Account

Permanently Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your NaijaJobs account and its associated information.

Important: This action cannot be undone.

When you delete your account, the following information will be removed:

  • Your profile and account information.
  • Your job listings and applications.
  • Your conversations and messages.
  • Your reviews and ratings.
  • Your notifications and account preferences.
  • Uploaded profile pictures and associated application documents.
  • Your support tickets and messages.

Financial transaction records and legal acceptance records may be retained where required for record-keeping.

If you are a client, application documents uploaded by workers for your jobs will also be deleted.
